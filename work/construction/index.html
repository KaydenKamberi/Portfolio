The brief

A local handyman needed a simple, fast-loading website to showcase his work and generate phone calls. The site had to be easy to navigate, clearly display his services and service area, and work well on mobile devices for customers searching on their phones. No complex features, just a straightforward presentation of what he does and how to contact him.

What I built

A four-page static site with a clean, bold design that puts the phone number front and center. The home page features a hero with a clear value proposition, a scrolling testimonial marquee, and photo tiles of recent work. Services are listed with checkmarks, and the work page shows before/after photo pairs. All photos are self-hosted WebP under 200 KB, and the site uses self-hosted fonts to eliminate third-party requests. A sticky call bar at the bottom ensures the phone number is always accessible.

Key features

  1. Mobile-first responsive design with large tap targets
  2. Photo gallery with before/after comparisons
  3. Scrolling testimonial marquee for social proof
