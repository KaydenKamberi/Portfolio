The brief

Marisol needed a site that matched the warmth and craft of its food: handwritten chalkboards, turquoise tiles, and the smell of wood smoke. The owner wanted the online menu to feel like the paper one left on each table, the locations page to double as a reservation hub, and the whole experience to load fast on the slow cafe Wi-Fi.

What I built

A four-page responsive site that leans on Tailwind for layout and a custom CSS layer for the brand's handmade feel. The hero pairs a big serif headline with a full-bleed photo of the La Jolla deck; the menu page groups dishes into visual grids with prices in the brand's coral; locations are full-screen cards with photos of each space and direct "Reserve" CTAs; and the about page tells the origin story over a wood-fire photo. The header shrinks on scroll, and the whole site weighs under 1.2 MB.

Key features

  1. Scrolling menu with grouped categories and price display
  2. Location pages with integrated reservation system
  3. Responsive design that works on phones and tablets
