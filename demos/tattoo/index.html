Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting tattooed

Tattoo pricing varies based on size, complexity, and the artist's hourly rate. Most tattoos range from $150-$500, with larger pieces priced accordingly. We provide quotes during your consultation.

Pain levels vary from person to person and depend on the location. Most clients describe it as a discomfort rather than pain. We use high-quality equipment and techniques to minimize discomfort.

Get a good night's sleep, eat a meal before your appointment, stay hydrated, and avoid alcohol 24 hours before. Wear comfortable clothing that allows easy access to the tattoo area.

We provide detailed aftercare instructions and recommend washing with mild soap, applying healing ointment, and avoiding direct sunlight. We'll schedule a follow-up to check on your healing progress.

For safety reasons, we do not tattoo clients who are pregnant or nursing. We recommend waiting until after you've finished nursing to get tattooed.

Yes, our artists are experienced in cover-up work. During your consultation, we'll assess your existing tattoo and discuss design options that will effectively cover it while creating something you'll love.
