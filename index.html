Process

Four steps from first message to launch day.

  1. Talk

    A short call about your business, your customers and what a new site needs to do for you.

  2. Design

    You see a homepage design first and give feedback before a single line of code is written.

  3. Build

    I hand-code the full site, write the page basics for Google, and test it on real phones.

  4. Launch

    Your site goes live on your domain, leads start landing in your inbox, and I stay on hand for tweaks.
